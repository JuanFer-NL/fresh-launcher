Fresh Launcher is a minimal home screen for Android: a big clock, the weather and a handful of favorites. Everything else is one swipe away.

Features:
- Two-tone clock with the date and current weather.
- Up to 7 favorites as plain text. Long-press and drag to reorder, or long-press and release to remove.
- App drawer with search that ignores accents and case. Swipe up or tap Apps, then press Go on the keyboard to open the first result.
- 7 mesh-gradient wallpapers with light and dark variants that follow the system theme. Optionally rotate them every 1-30 days.
- English and Spanish, system 12/24-hour time, and support for the system's Remove animations setting.
- Settings for language, Celsius or Fahrenheit, and whether the keyboard opens with the drawer.

No ads, analytics, trackers or accounts. Weather is optional: location is requested only after you tap Tap for weather. Coordinates are rounded to two decimals (about 1 km) before being sent to Open-Meteo for the forecast.

The first time it opens, Fresh Launcher asks whether to also use its gradient as your phone's wallpaper (home and lock screens). It makes going back home look smoother, but replaces your current wallpaper. If you keep yours, the gradient is shown only inside the app. You can change this anytime in Settings.

Requires Android 8.0 or later. Per-app language selection requires Android 13 or later.
